Master audio is shared across every scene — whatever is here and not muted is recorded no matter which scene is on the stage. Each source has its own volume, MUTE, and MON toggle (whether you hear it — it's always recorded). Keep MON off for your own microphone to avoid feedback. Hotkeys
Click a key box, then press the key you want. Esc clears it. New scenes get the next free key automatically. Reserved: Space (start/stop), R (record), M (mute), F (fullscreen), H (panel).
Config
Save config writes your whole setup to a file: scenes (type, name, hotkey, main source, members, positions), master and per-scene audio (volume / MUTE / MON), output settings, and hotkeys. Load config restores it in a new session — sources are re-attached by device name, so there's no need to re-arrange, re-add, or remap anything. Monitors / windows may need one re-pick if their names changed.
About
Capturra release 071526A. Save your setup with Settings → Config; the record location is remembered by the desktop app.
Running in a browser: monitors/system audio go through the browser's share picker, recordings save to Downloads when you stop, and permission prompts are file:// policy. The desktop app lists monitors by name, streams recordings to disk crash-safely, and never prompts.
